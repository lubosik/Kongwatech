# Kongwa Tech SEO and information strategy

## Primary entity

Kongwa Tech is a technology and strategy holding company. The canonical entity description is:

> Kongwa Tech partners with founders and brands to build, scale and operate ventures across AI, digital infrastructure and community.

Use Organization schema for the company and Person schema for Lubosi Kongwa. Do not use LocalBusiness or Service schema as the primary company identity.

## Canonical public routes

- `/` targets the company category, thesis and operating model.
- `/consult` is the sole corporate conversion route.
- `/ai-stack` is an editorial tools page with transparent affiliate disclosures.
- `/team/lubosi-kongwa` establishes the founder entity.
- `/blog` and its articles retain useful educational search equity.

Retired service, package, application, location and partner-list routes redirect permanently to the closest current destination and are excluded from the sitemap.

## Content principles

- Publish evidence-led thinking on product development, AI implementation, platform engineering, audience growth and venture operations.
- Use `Selected Ventures & Operating Work` when the underlying economic relationship is not public or verified.
- Do not imply that every partnership is an equity holding.
- Keep article CTAs aligned to the single corporate consultation goal.
- Do not reintroduce newsletter capture or local-service landing pages.
- Maintain descriptive titles, canonical URLs, Organization/Person/Article structured data and accurate sitemaps.
