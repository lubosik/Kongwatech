# Kongwa Tech Brand and Information Architecture

## Purpose

This document is the implementation copy deck for repositioning Kongwa Tech from a boutique AI consultancy to a technology and strategy holding company. The public experience should feel selective, operational and credible. It should explain how Kongwa Tech creates value without presenting packages, a service menu or an unverified investment portfolio.

Source-of-truth positioning:

> Kongwa Tech is a technology and strategy holding company that partners with founders and brands to build, scale, and operate ventures at the intersection of AI, digital infrastructure, and community. We take equity positions in select projects and deploy hands-on capability across product development, AI implementation, platform engineering, and audience growth. We do not take clients. We take stakes.

## Brand rules

### Category

Use:

- Technology and strategy holding company
- Venture partner
- Operating partner or operating partnership
- Ventures, partnerships and operating work
- Product, AI, infrastructure and audience capability

Do not use:

- Consultancy or consultant
- Agency
- Services or packages
- Freelance, retainer or hourly language
- Limited client slots
- Local Kent or Southeast England service positioning
- Apply Now

### Voice

- Direct, calm and specific
- Ambitious without inflated claims
- More operator than adviser
- Short sentences and disciplined section lengths
- UK English
- No generic claims such as “industry-leading”, “world-class”, “revolutionary” or “end-to-end”
- No invented metrics, discounts, investment amounts or portfolio valuations

### Relationship language

“We take equity positions in select projects” is a company-level statement. It must never be turned into a claim that every named company or project is owned by, invested in by or held by Kongwa Tech.

Use relationship-specific labels:

- Partnership
- Operating work
- Growth infrastructure
- Product system
- Selected venture

Do not use these labels unless separately verified for that specific company:

- Portfolio company
- Kongwa Tech company
- Investment
- Holding
- Equity stake
- Acquired by Kongwa Tech

Use this note beneath named work:

> Relationships vary by project. Inclusion here does not imply an investment or equity holding.

## Recommended public information architecture

The primary corporate experience should be a focused homepage supported by only a few purposeful routes.

### Desktop navigation

1. Thesis: `/#thesis`
2. Capabilities: `/#capabilities`
3. Ventures: `/#ventures`
4. AI Stack: `/ai-stack`
5. About: `/#about`
6. Consult About Your Project: `/consult` (visually primary)

### Mobile navigation

1. Thesis
2. Ventures
3. AI Stack
4. About
5. Consult About Your Project (visually primary)

Capabilities remains discoverable through the homepage scroll and does not need to make the mobile menu longer.

### Route decisions

| Route | Decision | Public role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Rewrite | Primary company narrative and conversion path |
| `/consult` | Create | Selective project enquiry |
| `/ai-stack` | Create | Operator-led tools and affiliate destination |
| `/team/lubosi-kongwa` | Retain and rewrite | Founder profile |
| `/blog` and useful articles | Retain | Rename publicly to Insights; remove newsletter and package promotion |
| `/partners` | Consolidate and redirect to `/#ventures` | Public partnerships can appear as a compact relationship strip on the homepage |
| `/team` and `/about` | Consolidate and redirect to `/#about` or the founder profile | Avoid duplicate thin company pages |
| `/services` and all service/package routes | Retire and redirect | No service catalogue remains |
| `/apply` | Permanently redirect to `/consult` | No application/package framing remains |
| Local consultant routes | Permanently redirect to `/` | No local consultancy landing pages remain indexable |

Keep the corporate site to one conversion goal: **Consult About Your Project**. The button may appear in the hero, navigation and final section, but its label and destination should not change.

Blog/Insights should not compete for a primary navigation slot. It can remain in the footer and continue to receive search traffic.
